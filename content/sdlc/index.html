The "After" — software engineering becomes factory engineering

Distilled from Zach Lloyd (Warp), "Self-Improving Software Factories: The New Open Source Model" — AI Engineer World's Fair 2026. source talk. This is the "where it's heading" capstone that sharpens the contrast — the verified research nodes above are the "before"; this is the concrete vision of the "after."

"Software engineering will become factory engineering." …and "You won't be building the product. You'll be building the thing that builds the product." The engineer's locus shifts from writing code to designing and operating the factory that writes code.

The factory loop — an agent at every stage of the SDLC

↻ Outer loop (self-improvement): a skill-updater agent reviews outcomes & comments, then opens PRs that improve the other agents' SKILL.mds — the factory compounds on itself. No pre-2022 analog exists for this.

Four pillars of an effective factory

Automation

Work moves itself through the pipeline — agents triage, spec, implement, review, and verify.

Context + Skills

Reusable, retrievable skills + RAG give agents the repo's knowledge, conventions, and tool access.

Human-in-the-loop

Humans review specs, code, and verification output — now the rate-limiting, highest-leverage step.

Self-improvement

Inner loop ships software; outer loop upgrades the skills. The factory gets better at building itself.

The new unit of measurement

Then · ≤ 2022
DORA & SPACE measured human throughput — deploy frequency, lead time, MTTR, change-failure rate; plus activity, communication, efficiency & satisfaction.
Now · factory era
Factory efficiency = Software shipped ÷ (token cost + human cost)A new denominator appears: AI token spend joins human time on the bottom line.
